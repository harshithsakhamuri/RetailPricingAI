🏷️ Retail Pricing AI

Pick a product and we'll suggest the price that should earn you the most profit. We also tell you how sure we are and why.

New here? How this works (1 minute read)
1. We learn from past salesWe studied a year of real sales from an online gift shop: how much of each product sold, and what happened when prices changed.
2. We predict next weekWe estimate how many units each product will sell next week, and how that number would change if you raised or lowered the price.
3. We find the best priceA higher price earns more per sale but usually means fewer sales. We look for the price where the two balance out and you keep the most profit.
4. We play it safeWe never suggest changing a price by more than 20%, never go far above a price you've charged before, and always keep at least a 20% profit margin.

Profit = what you keep after paying for the product = (selling price − your cost) × number sold. The suggestions are for the week of , right after the sales records end.
